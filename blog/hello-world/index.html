This is the first post on my site. It feels appropriate to start with something like this.

Who I am

My name is Serhii Yahdzhyiev. I go by [SY]. I’m a software engineer based in Potsdam, Germany, and I’ve been writing music, producing tracks, and playing drums since I was a teenager.

These two things — code and music — have always coexisted in my life, never quite competing. They both require the same thing: sustained attention, pattern recognition, and the willingness to iterate until something feels right.

What this site is

A place to write. About software, about production, about the overlap between the two. No particular schedule, no particular format. Just things I find worth writing down.

What to expect

Some posts will be technical. Some won’t. All of them will be honest.

If any of it resonates — you know where to find me.
