Contributing across the full software development lifecycle - from requirements analysis and architecture design to implementation, testing, and deployment. Placing strong emphasis on reproducibility, maintainability, and transparency of results. Primary interests: data-driven applications that leverage modern algorithms to automate and digitalize complex processes.
